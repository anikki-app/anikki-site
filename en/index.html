What Anikki does

  • Bring your list in seconds

    Import your MyAnimeList export. Status, progress and dates are there right away.

  • Rate the way you feel

    Story, animation, music, characters and more: your ratings build your personal taste profile.

  • Never miss a new episode

    Anikki lets you know as soon as a new episode has aired in Japan. One tap marks it as watched.

  • Home screen widgets

    What you are watching, right on your home screen. Tap +1 for the next episode without opening the app.

  • Where to watch

    For many titles, one tap takes you straight to the streaming service.

  • Discover this season

    What is airing now, what comes next, and filters by genre, year and format.

Your list belongs to you. No account needed, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
